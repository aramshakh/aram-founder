Aram Shakhbandaryan is the founder and CEO of Empy.ai, a company building AI tools that help people communicate better in high-stakes conversations. His work sits at the intersection of applied AI, product strategy, psychology, and go-to-market execution, with a focus on helping people think, speak, listen, and decide better while important conversations are still happening.

Before founding Empy, Aram worked at Google as an Analytical Consultant supporting major B2B SaaS and fintech clients across multiple regions. Across his career, he has worked in product, growth, analytics, and launch roles at companies including Google, adidas, Artec 3D, Krisp, and other technology businesses.

What makes his perspective distinctive is the combination of enterprise analytics, product and GTM execution, and a long-term interest in communication, trust, and human behavior. He is especially interested in how AI can move from passive reporting and summaries into real-time support during difficult human conversations such as sales calls, fundraising meetings, hiring interviews, and leadership discussions.
