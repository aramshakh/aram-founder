Aram Shakhbandaryan is the founder of Decato, a startup building AI tools that turn floor plans into buyable interior concepts using real products, layout-aware workflows, and faster revision loops for designers.

Previously, Aram founded Empy.ai, a conversation intelligence startup focused on high-stakes human communication. Before that, he worked at Google as an Analytical Consultant supporting major B2B SaaS and fintech clients across multiple regions. Across his career, he has worked in product, growth, analytics, and launch roles at companies including Google, adidas, Artec 3D, Krisp, and other technology businesses.

What makes his perspective distinctive is the combination of enterprise analytics, product and go-to-market execution, and a systems-level view of how AI should fit into real professional workflows. He is especially interested in products that move from inspiration or reporting into usable output that helps people decide and ship faster.
