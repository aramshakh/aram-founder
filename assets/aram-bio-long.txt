Aram Shakhbandaryan is co-founder and CEO of Decato, which turns a floor plan into a finished room you can actually buy: real products, real dimensions, real prices. The hard part was never the image. It is keeping the geometry, the fit and the real product constraints true all the way from the plan to the specification a supplier can quote. He builds it with his co-founder Vladimir Borisov.

Before Decato he spent four years on Empy.ai, real-time coaching for high-stakes calls. It reached 4,000 users and a $155K pre-seed, ran seven US enterprise pilots, went through Draper University and Plug and Play Armenia, and closed in 2026. Alongside it, from 2020 to 2024, he ran a communication training practice for senior managers in technology: fourteen four-day residential programmes and over three hundred people trained. Decato is the next attempt on a much narrower wedge.

Before founding companies he spent a decade on other people's budgets: analytical consultant at Google from 2020 to 2021 across the US, EU and APAC; product management at inKin; senior marketing manager at Krisp; product marketing team lead at Cryptology in Singapore; product marketing at Artec 3D; and performance marketing at adidas. He holds a bachelor's in marketing from the Russian State University for the Humanities, spent two years in the psychoanalysis master's programme at the Higher School of Economics, and went through Draper University's Startup Hero programme in 2024. He has been a member of the First Generation Foundation, a private network of founders and operators founded in Moscow in 2012, since 2015. He is based in Yerevan.
