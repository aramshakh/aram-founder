Aram Shakhbandaryan is co-founder and CEO of Decato, which turns a floor plan into a room a designer can actually order. The hard part was never the image. It is keeping the geometry, the fit and the real product constraints true all the way from the plan to the spec sheet. He builds it with his co-founder Vladimir Borisov.

Before Decato he spent four years on Empy.ai, communication intelligence for high-stakes calls. It reached 4,000 users and a $165K pre-seed, went through Draper University and Plug and Play Armenia, and closed in 2026. Decato is the next attempt on a much narrower wedge.

Before founding companies he spent a decade on other people's budgets: analytical consultant at Google from 2020 to 2021 across the US, EU and APAC; product management at inKin; marketing lead at Cryptology in Singapore; and performance marketing at Krisp, Artec 3D and adidas. He holds a master's in psychoanalysis from the Higher School of Economics and a bachelor's in marketing from the Russian State University for the Humanities, and went through Draper University's Startup Hero programme in 2024. He has been a member of the First Generation Foundation, a closed Russian community of founders and operators, since 2015. He is based in Yerevan.
