## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are journalists, podcast hosts, event organizers, investors, founder peers, and warm intros who need a fast, credible sense of who Aram is. Secondary users are potential partners, candidates, and customers who want a clean founder-facing overview without digging through LinkedIn or a long deck.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, sharp. YC-style simplicity with founder warmth. The page should feel intelligent, human, and current — not corporate, not flashy, not crypto, not agency-polished.

### Aesthetic Direction
Simple founder page with strong typography, generous whitespace, restrained color, and one memorable element: a warm human portrait paired with crisp positioning. Light mode first. Slight editorial feel. References: YC / Linear / good personal founder pages. Anti-reference: dark neon AI aesthetic, glassmorphism, gradient-heavy startup pages, overdesigned agency landing pages.

### Design Principles
1. Clarity first: every section should answer a real press-kit question fast.
2. Human before hype: show Aram as a person, not just a startup label.
3. Quiet confidence: minimal but premium, with typography doing most of the work.
4. Proof over puffery: use selected highlights and concrete facts instead of inflated claims.
5. Shareability: should work equally well as a public founder page, a speaker page, and a lightweight press kit.
