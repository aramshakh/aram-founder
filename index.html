Aram Shakhbandaryan is the founder of Decato, a new startup building AI tools for interior design workflow. Decato focuses on turning floor plans into furnished, buyable room concepts using real products, layout constraints, and faster revision loops.

Previously, Aram founded Empy.ai, a conversation intelligence startup focused on high-stakes human communication. Before that, he worked at Google as an Analytical Consultant supporting large B2B SaaS and fintech clients across multiple regions. Across his career, he has worked in product, growth, analytics, and launch roles at companies including Google, adidas, Artec 3D, Krisp, and other technology businesses.

What makes his perspective distinctive is the combination of enterprise analytics, product and go-to-market execution, and a systems-level view of how AI should fit into real professional workflows. He is especially interested in products that move from inspiration or reporting into usable output that helps people decide and ship faster.

Decato

“Decato turns floor plans into buyable interior concepts built around real products, fit-aware layout logic, and faster revisions for designers.”
