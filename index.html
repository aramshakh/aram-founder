Founder bio

Human before hype.

A clean summary for press, podcasts, events, investor intros, and warm founder shares.

Aram Shakhbandaryan is the founder and CEO of Empy.ai, where he is building AI tools that help people communicate better in high-stakes conversations.

Before founding Empy, Aram worked at Google as an Analytical Consultant supporting large B2B SaaS and fintech clients across multiple regions. Across his career, he has worked in product, growth, analytics, and launch roles at companies including Google, adidas, Artec 3D, Krisp, and other technology businesses.

What makes his perspective distinctive is the combination of three worlds that rarely sit together in one profile: enterprise analytics, product and go-to-market execution, and a long-term interest in psychology, communication, and trust. He is especially interested in how AI can move from passive reporting into real-time support during difficult human conversations.

Company

Empy.ai

Conversation intelligence designed to help while the call is still happening.

“Empy’s mission is to help people have better high-stakes conversations by turning AI into real-time support for clarity, attention, and trust.”

Empy.ai builds AI tools for high-stakes conversations such as sales calls, fundraising meetings, hiring interviews, leadership conversations, and other moments where clarity and attention matter. Instead of focusing only on notes or post-call summaries, Empy is designed to help during the conversation itself.
