About

Now

I build software that holds the rules of a messy situation. Right now that means rooms.

Decato takes a floor plan and a brief, lays out an editable room, sources real products that actually fit, and assembles a client-ready deck. The interesting part is not the render. It is that everything in the room can be bought, and that it fits.

Before this

I spent four years on Empy.ai. It started as a Slack bot that caught conflict in team chat and became communication intelligence for high-stakes calls.

We worked with 43 companies across pilots and subscriptions, raised a $165K pre-seed, ran US pilots with Erickson Coaching International and Facility Solutions Group, and hit second product of the day on Product Hunt. I closed it in 2026. Decato is the next attempt, on a much narrower wedge.

Before that, Google from 2020 to 2021, on accounts including Semrush, Miro, Veeam and Wrike. Earlier still: product management at inKin, marketing lead at Cryptology in Singapore, and performance work at Krisp, Artec 3D and adidas.

The thread

At eighteen I ran a small digital agency, websites and marketing for whoever would pay, while I was still at school.

Before that I was building Warcraft III custom maps. You take an engine someone else wrote and build a situation inside it with its own physics, then let other people live in there.

Empy did that for conversations. Decato does it for rooms. It has always been the same work.

The step between games and conversations is easy to miss. While I was at Google I took a master's in psychoanalysis at the Higher School of Economics and finished it in 2021. A year later I was training a model to tell an evaluation apart from an observation in someone's speech.

What I am good at

Finding the rule underneath a mess. Empy had to tell an evaluation apart from an observation in live speech. Decato has to keep a room dimensionally true all the way from the floor plan to the spec sheet. A consulting firm hired me to map a manual audit process end to end before any of it could be automated, across screenshot capture, knowledge retrieval, hypothesis generation and brief assembly. It is the same job every time.

Getting strangers to try an unfinished thing. 43 companies came through Empy pilots and subscriptions. I have pitched investors roughly 200 times over Zoom. Thirty people gave up a Saturday to put an agent on their own server because I asked them to. I am not selling the product, I am selling the chance to try it.

Running the economics. Ten years on other people's budgets before I risked my own: up to $3.9M a quarter at adidas, six markets at Artec 3D, five enterprise accounts at Google. I know what a number has to do before it is worth reporting.

Shipping into markets I do not live in. The US, UK, Canada, China, South Korea, Japan, Singapore, Latin America. From Moscow, then Singapore, then San Francisco, now Yerevan.
