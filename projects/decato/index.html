Decato

2026 to now · running

Interior design workflow software, co-founded with Vladimir Borisov. It takes a floor plan and a brief, lays out an editable room, sources products that exist and fit the dimensions, and assembles a specification a client can approve and a supplier can quote.

The hard part was never the image. It is keeping the geometry, the fit and the real product constraints true all the way from the plan to the spec sheet.

The counterintuitive thing we found while working out what to sell is that a better picture can cost the client money: a render that looks finished raises what contractors quote against it. That pushed us away from selling images and towards selling the check — does it fit, does it exist, what does it really cost — at the moment the designer is choosing.

Where it is

Fifty users to date. The average first session runs about six hours. First paying customer in the US. Last checked 5 September 2026.
