Empy.ai

December 2021 to May 2026 · closed

Conversation AI for high-stakes calls, co-founded and led as CEO. It began as a transformer fine-tuned to separate evaluation from observation in written speech at 82% F1, shipped as a Slack bot for team communication, and became a macOS application that ran during live calls.

Numbers

Four thousand users. A $165,000 pre-seed. 43 companies through pilots and subscriptions. US pilots with Erickson Coaching International and Facility Solutions Group. Three Product Hunt launches; the best came second product of the day with 203 upvotes.

Programmes and stages

Draper University's Startup Hero programme in 2024, then Plug and Play Armenia. One of nine Burning Heroes finalists in San Francisco that year, which meant a live pitch at Plug and Play Silicon Valley.

How it ended

The Electron client had stability problems that cost retention and was rewritten in Swift in February 2026. That fixed the crashes, not the business. Closed in May 2026. The product site is still up at empy.ai.
