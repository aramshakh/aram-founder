Founder loneliness is its own species

May 24, 2025 · translated from Russian

Founder loneliness is its own species.

You are not simply alone in a room. You are carrying an idea through fog while other people watch carefully from the side. You believe, you put in the last of what you have, and next to you there are transparent silhouettes: they nod, they do not risk anything. The one who walks into fire and water with you feels the same vibrations. That is a real co-author of the route.

Skin in the game decides it. If your partner puts the same amount on the table, the energy synchronises. Every exhale, every dip is read instantly, and instead of pity you get someone saying let's go one more round.

More often what you get is people making a minimum safety deposit. The moment the horizon shakes, you are back alone with the echo of your own thoughts. So many projects die exactly here, somewhere between we will support you once you take off and you were so close.

When the last light goes out, the question left is simple: who jumps after you if this thing goes down?
