Pushy and fast is the best combination there is

August 29, 2025 · translated from Russian

Being pushy and quick to learn is the best combination there is.

Today is the second day of non-stop spamming and showing Empy to users.

Yesterday only two people managed to install it.
The app broke twice while I was demoing to investors.

I drew my conclusion, exhaled. Went and fixed the instructions, started doing onboardings on calls.
Got the first feedback from users.

I spammed about eight group chats asking for help testing, and twenty-something people in DMs.
I was not selling the product. I was selling the chance to try the product.

To see who in my circle responds and gives feedback.
To check who answers and who stays quiet.
