Empy is not about focus

May 1, 2025 · translated from Russian

Empy is not about focus.
It is about how we stopped being in contact.

Empy did not arrive as a product from a market.
It was born out of pain.
Out of the feeling of not being heard.
Of not hearing.
Of being supposedly in the conversation, and absent from it.

It did not start with an idea. It started with me.
With dozens of calls where I caught myself switching off.
With sessions where I was talking and not in contact.
With attempts to build teams that fell apart not over skills, but over the impossibility of actually being together.

And at some point the shift became obvious:
the problem is not outside.
The answers are not outside.
The solution is not in the structure.

You cannot assemble a system while you are a fragment inside.
You cannot hear another person while you are out of focus yourself.
And then I understood:

We do not know how to be together. Not on a call. Not in life.
We do not know how to be alive inside a conversation.

That is where the real pain is.
And that is where Empy came from.

Empy is not about productivity.
Empy is a product, but not for the sake of a feature.

It is a tool that helps bring contact back.
With yourself. With the other person. With the moment.
An interface you do not hide behind.
Where you hear.
Where you are heard.
Where a conversation is not just words but a space you can stay in.

We are entering a market almost nobody sees:
the market of human presence.
The market of live interaction.
The market of being with another person without losing yourself.

We are not just building a product.
We are working on the thing that breaks interaction for millions of people every day:
the inability to be present.
the absence of presence.
powerlessness in communication.
